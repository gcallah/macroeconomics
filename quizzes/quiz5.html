Name: ____________________________________________________

1) Two major contributors to our well-being that are left out of GDP are:

a) factory production and car sales

b) shoe manufacturing and toothpaste

c) heroin and crack

d) the environment and domestic (in the home) production

2) One reason certain important things are left out of the measure of GDP is:

a) economists are embarrassed to talk about sex

b) car sales are not considered “real” economic activity

c) environmental issues and domestic production are hard to measure

d) shoes are beneath economists

Consider the following small nation of Callahandia:

There are 1000 residents.

300 are under 16.

200 are engaged in unpaid childcare at their own home or are retired, and are not looking for work.

400 are working.

100 are searching for jobs.

3) The unemployment rate in Callahandia is:

a) 10%

b) 20%

c) 40%

d) 60%

4) The size of the labor force is:

a) 400

b) 500

c) 600

d) 700

5) The labor force participation rate is:

a) 40%

b) 50%

c) 61.4%

d) 71.4%

6) In the classical framework, if unemployment is too high, the solution is for:

a) food prices to fall

b) wages to fall

c) inflation to drop

d) the government to pass a stimulus package

7) In the Keynesian framework, if wages drop:

a) investment will drop still more

b) everyone will find employment

c) prices will rise

d) inflation will skyrocket

8) In the US labor statistics, people in prison are counted as:

a) unemployed

b) employed by the prison

c) out of the labor force

d) really bad-assed

9) An example of frictional unemployment is when:

a) someone switches careers and is looking for a job in his/her new field

b) the economy crashes, creating lots of friction, and millions lose their jobs

c) someone loses their job because they slipped on the ice due to a lack of friction

d) typewriters are replaced by computers, causing job losses among typewriter mechanics

10) An example of structural unemployment is when:

a) the economy crashes and millions lose their jobs

b) a teenager enters the labor force and spends time finding his/her first job

c) typewriters are replaced by computers, causing job losses among typewriter mechanics

d) someone slips on the ice, causing structural damage, and they lose their job
