PPF:

Here is a table of points along the production possibility frontier for basketball versus academic production:

Alternative Points per game Grade point average
A 20.0 0.0
B 17.5 2.0
C 15.0 3.0
D 10.0 3.75
E 0.0 4.0

  1. Graph this table as a PPF. You may put either class on either axis.

  2. Is it possible for this student to get a 3.2 GPA and score 15.0 points per game?

  3. Let’s say the student got a 3.75 GPA last semester. What is the opportunity cost of raising that grade to 4.0 in terms of points per game in basketball?

  4. Let’s imagine the student getting a 3.4 GPA and averaging 9 points per game. What can we say about that level of production?

  5. The student gets vitamin therapy that makes him smarter and stronger. What happens to the PPF?
