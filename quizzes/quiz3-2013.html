Suppose we have an economy with the consumption equation C = 25 + .8Y, and II = 55. Please fill out the table below, and then diagram the Keynesian cross that depicts this situation.

Y* AC* NAC* C* II* AD* S*
0
100
200
300
400
500
600

*Y =Income

AC = Autonomous consumption

NAC = Non-autonomous consumption, the part of consumption that depends upon the marginal propensity to consume

C = Total consumption

II = Intended investment

AD = Aggregate demand

S = Savings
