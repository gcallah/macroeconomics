Macroeconomics: Quiz 4

Name: ____________________________________________________

1) In a perfectly competitive market, there are:

a) many buyers and sellers

b) all units of the good being traded are identical

c) everyone has perfect information

d) all of the above

2) If buyers purchase less of a good in response to a change in price, that is called:

a) a drop in demand

b) a demand curve

c) a change in quantity demanded

d) all of the above

3) A decrease in supply is when:

a) the supply curve shifts to the left

b) the price of a good drops

c) the price of a good goes up

d) the supply curve shifts to the right

4) Ceteris paribus, a surplus of a good causes:

a) the price to drop

b) the demand curve to shift leftward

c) suppliers to produce more of the good

d) perfect competition

5) An example of a good typically thought to have a relatively inelastic demand is:

a) macaroni

b) tobacco

c) apple cider

d) cherry juice

6) A speculative bubble occurs when:

a) people buy an asset because they believe the price will keep shooting up in the short term

b) people buy an asset because they feel it is a fundamentally sound value

c) there is a large recession

d) there is great price stability in a market

7) The most common measure of a nation’s output is called:

a) BEA

b) NIPA

c) SNA

d) GDP

8) For the most part, national accounts include __________ in the accounting of national assets:

a) manufactured assets

b) human capital

c) natural resources

d) all of the above

9) In accounting for the value of government-produced goods, economic agencies often use:

a) accounting identities

b) wild guesses

c) imputation

d) SNA

10) If the dollar value of output doubles while prices also double, real GDP:

a) has doubled as well

b) has remained constant

c) has fallen to half of what it was

d) too little information
