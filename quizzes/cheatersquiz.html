Cheaters Quiz

1) Suppose there is an increase in the supply of iPods. What would we expect to happen to the equilibrium price and quantity of iPods?

a) Price and quantity will both fall.

b) Price and quantity will both rise.

c) Price will fall, quantity will rise.

d) Price will rise, quantity will fall.

2) Suppose there is an increase in the demand for gardeners. What would we expect to happen to the equilibrium price and quantity of gardening services?

a) Price will rise, quantity will fall.

b) Price will fall, quantity will rise.

c) Price and quantity will both fall.

d) Price and quantity will both rise.

3) Suppose there is a decrease in the supply of oil. What would we expect to happen to the equilibrium price and quantity of oil?

a) Price and quantity will both fall.

b) Price will rise, quantity will fall.

c) Price will fall, quantity will rise.

d) Price and quantity will both rise.

4) Which one of the following goods would be expected to have the least elastic demand curve?

a) Budweiser beer

b) Purple flowers

c) An eight ball of blow

d) Vegetable oil

5) A charity promoting breast cancer research would be classified into which sector in the Bureau of Economic Analysis’ system of national accounts?

a) The government sector

b) The foreign sector

c) The business sector

d) The households and institutions sector
