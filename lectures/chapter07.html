12 Slide 12

  • The Keynesian View
  • The following may prevent adjustments:
    • Sticky wages—workers resist wage reductions
    • Insider-outsider—outsiders can be shut out by contracts and lack of cooperation
    • Efficiency wage—higher than equilibrium wages can motivate workers to work harder
